# Scene assets

- `macbook-pro-m5.glb`: supplied by the portfolio owner in `macbook-pro-14-inch-m5.zip`. Original mesh, textures, and material assignments retained; portfolio screen and hinge animation applied at runtime. No license was included in the supplied archive.
- `books/*.glb` and `books/*-cover.jpg`: baked by `scripts/bake-books` from three [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) scans, all [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license):
  - `experience`: the closed binder from [Binder Notebook](https://polyhaven.com/a/binder_notebook); the maker's emblem is removed and the title is gold-stamped.
  - `research`: volume book01 from [Book Encyclopedia Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/book_encyclopedia_set_01); dyed oxblood, the baked spine lettering lifted out and new titles tooled in gold.
  - `projects`: a hardcover (`book_hardcover_01_cover47`) from [Decorative Book Set 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/decorative_book_set_01), with a new cloth binding and gold blocking.
  - `writings`: a softcover (`book_softcover_01_cover82`) from the same set, with a new printed jacket.

  Original scanned meshes, normal and material maps are retained; texture islands are repacked so each book gets a full 2k atlas.
